Privacy policy

QRio keeps your codes on your phone.

This version of QRio has no accounts, no ads and no internet permission. What you scan and create stays inside the app on your device. This page explains the details.

Effective October 10, 2026. Covers QRio 1.0.0.

At a glance

Personal data collected
None. QRio has no accounts, sign-in, analytics or ads.
Internet access
QRio does not request the INTERNET permission, so the app cannot connect to the internet on its own.
Where your codes live
Only on your phone, in QRio's private app storage.
What you control
Delete any code or your whole history in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling.

What QRio stores on your device

To work, QRio keeps some information locally on your phone:

  • Codes you scan: the text, link or other content, and when you scanned it.
  • Codes you create: their content, design, name, favorites and folders.
  • Your settings, such as the theme and scanner options.
  • A PIN, if you turn on app lock.

Android keeps this data separate from other apps, and QRio does not receive a copy of it. If you have turned on Android's own backup, your phone may include app data in that backup. That is controlled by your Android settings, not by QRio.

Camera and photos

QRio uses the camera to read QR codes and barcodes. The camera image is processed on your phone while the scanner is open. QRio does not record video or save camera frames.

When you scan from a photo, you choose the image yourself. QRio reads the code in it, and does not upload the image or keep a copy of it.

Links and scan results

When you scan a link, QRio's Safe-link check runs on your phone. It looks at the address itself, for example whether it is shortened or uses plain HTTP, and it does not visit the link.

When you tap Open, your browser or another app opens the link, and that website's own privacy policy applies. Actions such as joining a WiFi network or adding a contact are handed to Android. QRio does not send those details anywhere.

Exporting and sharing

When you export or share a QR code, a CSV file or a PDF, it is saved where you choose or sent through Android's share menu to the app you pick. QRio cannot see who receives it. Anything inside a code, such as a WiFi password, travels with it, so share carefully.

Permissions

The permissions QRio asks for in this version are listed below.

Camera
To scan codes. Used only while the scanner is open.
Vibration
A short vibration when a code is scanned, if you leave it turned on in Settings.
Storage, Android 9 and older
To save exported images and files. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

Third parties

This version of QRio has no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no other third-party service that collects data. If a future version adds any of these, this page will be updated first and the app's store description will say so.

Children

QRio is a general-purpose tool and is not aimed at children under 13. Because QRio collects no personal information, it holds nothing about any user, children included.

Your choices

  • Delete individual codes, or clear your history, inside the app.
  • Turn off the PIN in Settings.
  • Remove a permission in Android Settings, under Apps, QRio, Permissions.
  • Uninstall QRio to remove all of its data from your phone.
